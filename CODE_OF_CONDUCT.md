# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:
- Be respectful and welcoming.
- Assume good intent.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted, in issues, pull requests or anywhere else in the project.

To report unacceptable behaviour, email **contact@almarfeld.com**. Reports are handled confidentially, and maintainers may remove content or block participants who break these rules.
